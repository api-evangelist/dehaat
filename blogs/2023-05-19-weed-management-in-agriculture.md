Weed control is a very vital aspect of farming that can not be neglected by the farmers if they want a healthy yield. There are various techniques and ways to control the weeds ensuring safe and healthy growth of the crops for enhanced productivity and quality. Weeds are undesirable plants for farmers that grow in the fields.
