Proper reach to markets can enable farmers to sell their agri-produce at a reasonable price within their capacity. Market access for any kind of business is very much required in order to sell their products and services. Without proper market access, no business can thrive in the long run as it will face substantial losses even though they produce the best products or provide excellent services.
